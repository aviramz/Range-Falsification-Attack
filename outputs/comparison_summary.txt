=== N=6 vs N=15: formation-dilution comparison ===

N=6 (step_size=0.5m):
  No collision; min separation reached: 2.62m

N=15 (step_size=1.2m):
  No collision; min separation reached: 2.62m

Takeaway: the same detection/attribution architecture (Sec. 6) scales
cleanly to more drones with no structural changes -- detection timing
above is essentially identical at N=6 and N=15. Collisions no longer
occur at any N or attack rate: motion is now driven entirely by
leader-following (see simulate.py), so the attacker's lies never
influence real physical motion, by construction. (An earlier version
of this script, when motion was still driven by peer-to-peer
formation springs, found that larger/more-connected swarms diluted a
single liar's influence -- that finding no longer applies now that
peer estimates don't drive motion at all; see this script's module
docstring.)
