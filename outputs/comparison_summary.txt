Takeaway: the same detection/attribution architecture (Sec. 6) scales
cleanly to more drones with no structural changes. Physical danger from
a FIXED attack rate does not scale the same way -- larger, more
connected swarms are more physically robust to a single falsified link
purely as a byproduct of full-mesh formation control (more honest
neighbors diluting one liar's influence on the total corrective signal).
This is a property of the control architecture, not of detection.
